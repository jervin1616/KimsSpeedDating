Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Kim's Speed Dating is a private tool the host and their team use to run a speed dating game on a live stream, plus a screen that lets viewers follow along. In this policy "we" means the host and their team. This page explains what information the tool handles and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Your Google sign-in. When you sign in with Google we receive your verified email address. We never see your Google password. We do not store your name or profile photo.
  • A sign-in record. For people who sign in but are not on the host's list we keep the email address, the first and last time you signed in, and how many times. We keep the latest 300 of these and drop the oldest.
  • The access list. The email addresses of people the host has approved, the role each one has, and who added or changed them and when. We also keep a short history of the last 100 changes so a mistake can be undone.
  • Information about people who take part in the show. A name or handle, age, state, gender, sexuality, the age range they are looking for, a note that they confirmed they are 18 or older, how long they spent in each seat, and which seat they are in. We also keep the match log (who picked whom) and summaries of past streams (counts and times).
  • Questions. The text of questions sent in from chat, the chat handle of the person who sent it if the host entered one, and how many times it has been asked.
  • What stays in your own browser. Your sign-in session, the tab you last looked at, and a note that you hid the "Install the app" card. We do not set cookies.

Sensitive information

Gender and sexuality are sensitive. We only record them for people who choose to take part in the show and who confirm they are 18 or older. The board that viewers see shows a seated person's name or handle, age, state, a short gender and sexuality label, and the age range they want. The same board is shown on the live stream. If you do not want that shown, please do not take part, or ask the host to remove you at any time.

How we use it

  • To run the show and keep the board, questions and match log up to date for everyone watching.
  • To decide who can sign in and what they can do (watch, help with questions, run the show, or manage access).
  • To keep the service safe and available, for example by limiting how fast one connection can send requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not run analytics or tracking, and we do not sell it.

Who can see what

  • Anyone who signs in can see the board, the current question, past stream summaries, and the question list including any chat handles attached to questions.
  • Helpers (contributors) can also add questions.
  • Hosts (editors) can see the full list of people who take part, the match log and past streams.
  • Owners can also see and change who has access, the sign-in record and the change history.

Who we share it with

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores the data. It runs on Cloudflare's network, which operates in many countries, so information may be processed outside the country you live in.
  • Google provides sign-in. The page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads. Google's own policies apply to what it does.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

  • Information about people who take part, questions, the match log and stream summaries stay until the host removes them.
  • The sign-in record keeps the latest 300 entries. The access change history keeps the last 100 changes.
  • Your browser session lasts up to 12 hours and renews quietly while the page is open, for up to 7 days. Signing out clears it from that device.
  • Hosts can download backups of the data. Owners are asked to keep those private and to delete old ones.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you took part in the show, we will remove your record and any match log entries that mention you. If you signed in, we will remove you from the sign-in record and the access list. Contact the host through the live stream or the community Discord server. If you live somewhere with extra privacy rights (for example the UK, the EU, California, Canada or Australia) we will honor reasonable requests under those rights, and you may also complain to your local privacy authority. Copies already downloaded as backups may take a little longer to delete.

Children

This service is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and if we learn we have, we will remove it.

Security

We protect your information with the measures described on our Security page. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute protection.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will say so in the community Discord server.

Contact

Message the host through the live stream or the community Discord server.
