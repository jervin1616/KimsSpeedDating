Privacy

Kim's Speed Dating host console. Last updated November 2026.

This site is a private tool that the host and their moderators use to run a speed dating game on a live stream. It is not open to the public.

What we collect

  • Your Google email address. When you sign in with Google we receive your verified email address and use it only to decide what you are allowed to do. The email addresses of people the host has approved, and who approved them, are kept in the access list. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not ask for access to your Google data.
  • Game information entered by the host or moderators. For people who take part as daters: a name or handle, age, state, gender, sexuality, preferred age range, and a note that they confirmed they are 18 or older. Also questions sent in by chat viewers (with their handle if given), the match log and stream summaries.

Who can see it

Dater details and the match log are visible only to editors approved by the host. Moderators can see and add questions. Anyone who signs in with a Google account can watch the board (which shows the name, age and state of the daters currently seated), see the current question, read the question list including the chat handles of the people who sent them, and use a private question picker. They cannot change anything or see dater details beyond what is on the board. Anyone who has not signed in sees nothing.

Where it is kept

We also keep the email address and sign-in times of people who sign in without being on the host's list, so the host can see who has been watching and give them more access. The host can delete these entries.

Data is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. Your sign-in is remembered in your own browser for a limited time so you do not have to sign in again on every visit. We do not sell data, show ads, or use trackers.

Removing your information

If you took part and want your details removed, or you are on the access list and want to be taken off, ask the host through the stream and they will delete it.
