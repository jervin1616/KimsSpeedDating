Start here

Before a stream
  1. Today tab: look at Pending dater sign-ups and approve the ones you want.
  2. Open the announcer and schedule the go-live time, at least an hour ahead.
  3. On the main screen, check the Daters and Questions tabs.
During a stream
  1. Tap an empty seat to pick someone. Type to search.
  2. Press Match, then tap the panelist the hot seat picked.
  3. Raid or fake accounts? Today tab, Lock the door.
After a stream
  1. End the stream from the stream bar so it saves a summary.
  2. Download a backup from the Backup page.
  3. If the door is locked, open it again.

On the main screen hosts and owners

  • Seats: tap an empty seat to open the picker. Search by name, state or username. Tap a person to seat them. Tap a seated person, then another seat, to move or swap.
  • Colors: green fits both, yellow is close (off by 1 to 2 years), red is 3 or more years off or a mismatch with the hot seat.
  • Bottom bar: Remove hot seat (with Undo) and Match (saves the pick to the Match log).
  • Questions tab: Random question with a category menu, Ask this on any question, Back, Clear question. Add chat questions in the box at the top.
  • Stream bar: start the stream when you go live and end it when you finish.

Sign-ups and merging

Viewers can add a TikTok username and sign up to be a speed dater. Nothing reaches the board until you approve it.

  • Approve (Today tab, or from the seat picker) adds them to your dater list. Tapping a sign-up in the seat picker approves and seats them in one move.
  • If a sign-up looks like someone already on your list, Is this person already on your list? opens with the likely matches and why. Tap Merge into this one, check the before and after, then Confirm merge. You keep their history. Undo is right there if it was wrong.
  • Someone with a different name? Use Merge with existing... and search your daters.
  • When someone changes their TikTok username, their linked dater renames itself unless you set the name by hand.

Check on camera. The app cannot prove a username belongs to the person who typed it, and the 18+ box is only a checkbox. If someone claims a name that is not theirs, use Release username in People.

Going-live messages

  • Tap Open the announcer on the Today tab (or Announce on the main screen). Pick the date and time, which is Eastern. Tick phones and Discord. Press Schedule it.
  • Three messages go out: 1 hour before, 30 minutes before, and "We are live now!".
  • Send me a test goes to your own phone only. Cancel schedule removes what has not been sent. Messages whose time has already passed are skipped, so schedule at least an hour ahead.
  • Phones reach only people who turned notifications on. Discord reaches everyone in the channel, so keep it on.

People and safety

  • Everyone who signs in joins Viewers automatically. You never need to add them.
  • Promote: use the role menu on someone's row. Remove takes away a role you gave. Recent changes (Safety tab) lists changes with Undo.
  • Block stops one account. A new account gets around it, so for a wave of accounts use Lock the door: new sign-ins from people you have not added are turned away, and everyone already watching stays.
  • Sign out everywhere ends someone's sessions on every device. They can sign in again unless you also block them.
  • Erase a person's records (Safety tab) is for removal requests. You see everything that mentions them and choose what to erase.

What each role can do

Owner
Everything an Editor can do, plus this page: deciding who gets in and what they can do. Owners are set by whoever manages the site, so they show as locked here.
Editor
Runs the show. Seats daters, adds, edits and removes daters, uses the match log, starts and ends streams, manages all questions (add, ask, remove, clear) and downloads backups. Cannot change who has access.
Contributor
Helps with questions. Sees the board and the current question, adds questions from chat, presses Random question and asks questions, and can go Back. Cannot see the daters list, the match log or past streams, change seats, remove questions or clear the question.
Blocked
No access at all. They see a plain "not on the list" message and any open page is closed right away. Owners cannot be blocked.
Viewer
Watches. Sees the board and the current question, and has a private "Random question for me" button that does not change the stream. Cannot change anything.

Keep it safe

  • Do not post or screenshot this page, the pending list or a backup file. They hold emails, usernames and ages.
  • Never share a key or the Discord link. They are set in Cloudflare only. If one is ever shown, tell whoever manages the site so it can be replaced.
  • 18 and over only. The checkbox is not proof, so you seeing the person is the real check.
  • Gender, sexuality, age and state show on the board once someone is seated. Ask first, and remove anyone who asks.
