Security

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This page explains how we protect the service and what to do if you find a problem.

How we protect it

  • Sign-in through Google. We never see or store your password. We check that each Google sign-in is genuine and meant for this service before we let anyone in.
  • Access is checked on the server, every time. What you can see and change depends on your role. Hiding a button is never the only protection, so changing something in your browser does not unlock anything.
  • Daters and the match log are only available to hosts. Helpers and viewers cannot read them, even by trying.
  • Encrypted in transit. Everything between your device and the service uses HTTPS.
  • Limits against abuse. There are caps on message size, document size and message rate, and a cap on how many viewers can connect at once. Hosts and helpers keep reserved places so a crowd cannot lock them out.
  • Careful handling of what people type. Names, questions and handles are always shown as plain text, never run as code.
  • Secrets are kept out of the code. Keys and the access list are stored as protected settings, not in the website files.
  • Sign-out works on the server. Signing out ends that session on our side too, so a copied login stops working. Owners can also sign someone out on every device.
  • Blocking and a lock for raids. Owners can block an account, and can lock the door so new sign-ins are paused while people already watching keep watching.
  • Erasing on request. Owners have a tool that finds and removes a person's records when they ask.
  • Changes are recorded. Changes to who has access are logged, and an owner can undo a mistake.

What you can do

  • Sign out when you use a shared or borrowed device.
  • Do not share links or screenshots that show your browser's stored sign-in details.
  • Do not post personal details about other people.

Found a problem?

Please tell the host privately, by direct message in the community Discord server or through the host's usual contact. Please do not post it publicly until it has been fixed. Tell us what you saw, where, and how to repeat it. We welcome good-faith reports and will look into them as quickly as we can.

When you test, please use your own account and your own data only. Do not try to read or change other people's information, do not send large amounts of traffic, and do not interrupt a live stream.

Limits of this page

We work to keep the service secure, but no system is perfectly secure and we have not had an independent security audit. We cannot promise it will never be attacked or fail. If something goes wrong that affects your information, we will tell the people affected through the community Discord server as soon as we reasonably can.
