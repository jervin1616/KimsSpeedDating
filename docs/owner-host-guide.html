The short version

Hosts Editor

Run the board: seat daters, ask questions, press Match, start and end the stream. Everything you need is on the main screen.

Owners Kim, Eddey

Everything a host does, plus the Admin page (people, sign-ups, blocking) and the Announce button (going-live messages).

  1. An hour or more before: press Announce and schedule the go-live time.
  2. Open Admin and check Pending dater sign-ups.
  3. During the stream: tap an empty seat, search a name or username, tap the person.
  4. Ask questions from the Questions tab. Use the category menu next to Random question.
  5. If a raid or fake accounts show up: Admin, then Lock the door.

Before the stream

1
Schedule the announcement. Tap Announce (next to Admin), pick the date, leave the time at the one you want (it is Eastern time), and press Schedule it. Do this at least an hour ahead, or the 1 hour and 30 minute messages are skipped. See Going-live messages.
2
Check sign-ups. On the Admin page, the Pending dater sign-ups card lists everyone who asked to be a speed dater. Approve the ones you want. You can also leave them and approve people live from the seat picker.
3
Look at the dater list. On the main screen, open the Daters tab. Remove anyone who is not coming. Edit anything that is wrong.
4
Check the question box. Open the Questions tab. Make sure the category menu is there (if not, see Questions).
5
Start the stream from the stream bar at the top when you go live. End it when you finish. It saves a summary under Past streams.
Good habit

Download a backup from the Backup page after every stream. Keep the file private.

Running the show Hosts and owners

Putting someone in a seat

  • Tap an empty seat. A picker opens with your dater list. When the hot seat is filled, daters are sorted by how well they fit.
  • Search by name or state at the top. People who signed up but are not approved yet also show up here, marked Sign-up, but only when you type something.
  • Tap a person to seat them. Tapping a pending sign-up approves them and seats them in one move.
  • + Add new dater at the bottom adds someone by hand and seats them.
  • Tap a seated person, then another seat, to move or swap them.

The colors

Hearts turn green (fits both), yellow (close, off by 1 to 2 years) or red (off by 3 or more years, or an orientation mismatch) compared with whoever is in the hot seat. Tap a seat to see the reason.

The bottom bar

  • Remove hot seat takes the hot seat person out. An Undo appears for a few seconds.
  • Match: press it, then tap the panelist the hot seat picked. It saves the pick to the Match log.

Tabs

  • Daters: add, edit, remove, search and sort everyone.
  • Questions: see Questions.
  • Match log: every pick, with a way to remove one or clear them all.
  • Past streams: summaries of finished streams.

Sign-ups and merging

Viewers can add their TikTok username and sign up to be a speed dater. Nothing they enter reaches the board until you approve it.

Where sign-ups appear

  • Admin page, Pending dater sign-ups (owners): search by username or email. Buttons: Approve, Merge with existing..., Decline, and Clear all. It refreshes every 20 seconds.
  • Seat picker (hosts and owners): type a username and the sign-up appears in the same list.
  • Add dater form: if what you type looks like a sign-up, it asks Is this them?

Someone is already on your list

When a sign-up looks like a dater you already have (a similar name, a misspelling, a first name against a username), a window opens: Is @username already on your list?

  1. It lists up to six possible matches, with the reasons (for example "sounds alike, same state, same age").
  2. Tap Merge into this one on the right person. Not listed? Use the search box in the window.
  3. It shows exactly what changes, such as Age 30 → 31. Tap Confirm merge.
  4. You keep their history (panel and hot seat time, match log) and get their new details. Their old name stays searchable.
  5. Made a mistake? Tap Undo right after. It puts everything back.

Not the same person? Tap No, add as a new dater.

When someone changes their TikTok username

If their dater is linked to their account and still carries their old username, it renames itself. A name you set by hand is left alone. Old usernames still turn up in search, and the owner search shows "was @oldname".

Check on camera

The app cannot prove a TikTok username belongs to the person who typed it, and the 18+ box is only a checkbox. Confirm who someone is before you seat them. If someone claims a username that is not theirs, an owner can free it up (Admin, Find a person, Release username).

Going-live messages Owners only

1
Tap Announce at the top of the main screen.
2
Pick the date and time. The time is Eastern. It shows when the three messages will go out.
3
Tick Phone notifications and Post to Discord. Leave Ping @everyone off unless you want it.
4
Press Schedule it. You will see three lines, all Waiting.

Messages sent: "We go live in 1 hour", "30 minutes until we go live" and "We are live now!".

  • Send me a test sends one message to your own phone only.
  • Cancel schedule removes what has not been sent yet.
  • Scheduling again replaces the old schedule.
  • A message whose time has already passed is skipped, not sent late. Schedule at least an hour ahead to get all three.
Not everyone is reached

Phone notifications go only to people who turned them on in the app. On iPhone that means the app added to the home screen. Silent or Focus modes block them. The Discord post reaches everyone in the channel, so keep it on.

Questions

Asking

  • Random question picks one, and prefers ones that have been asked least. Use the menu next to it to limit it to a category, such as What would you do? or About your date.
  • Ask this (or Ask again) on any question in the Questions tab puts it on the stream. Back goes to the previous one. Clear question empties it.
  • Question from chat: type it in the box, add the @handle if you want, and press Add question. It goes under the category "From chat".
  • Hosts can Remove a question. The list shows 150 at a time, with Show more at the bottom.

Categories

Each question has a category. Use the filter above the list to browse one. Each row has a dropdown to fix a wrong category. Viewers see a category picker on their own random question button.

Owner setup, once

  • Question categories card on the Admin page: press Sort ... questions into categories if it says some are unsorted.
  • Question packs card: press Add ... questions to add the 265-question "Hot seat" set. Pressing it again does nothing, and your existing questions are never changed.

People and safety Owners only

Open Admin from the main screen. Everyone who signs in lands on a member list automatically as a Viewer. You do not need to add them.

Roles

  • Add or change someone: type their email, choose a role, press Save. People you add can sign in with that Google account.
  • Change a role from the dropdown on their row. Remove takes them off the list.
  • Recent changes lists the last changes with an Undo button, and a quick Undo bar appears at the top right after a change. Use it if someone's role was changed by accident.
  • Owners (you, Kim and Eddey) are locked on this page. They can only be changed in Cloudflare.

Dealing with trouble

Block

Stops one account completely: it cannot sign in or watch, and any open page is closed. They appear under Blocked with Unblock and an Undo in Recent changes. A person can make a new account, so for a wave of accounts use Lock the door.

Lock the door

Turns away new sign-ins from people who are on no list. People already watching keep watching, and anyone you added can still sign in. Turn it back off after the stream with Open the door.

Sign out everywhere

On any person's row. Ends their sessions on every device. They can sign in again, so also Block or Remove them if you want them gone.

Find a person

Search everyone who has signed in, by email or TikTok username (old usernames too). From there: Release username, Block, Sign out everywhere.

Someone asks to be removed

Use Erase a person's records on the Admin page. Type a name, handle or email. You see everything that mentions them. Untick anything that is not theirs, then press Erase the ticked items. It removes their daters, match log entries, seats, chat handles on questions and account records. Erasing a blocked person lifts the block, so it leaves blocked accounts unticked. Backups you already downloaded are not changed.

Keep it safe

  • Do not post or screenshot the Admin page, the pending list or a backup file. They hold emails, usernames and ages.
  • Never share a secret. Keys and the Discord webhook are set in Cloudflare only. If one is ever shown or pasted somewhere, tell the site manager so it can be replaced.
  • 18 and over only. The sign-up box is a checkbox, so the real check is you seeing the person.
  • Be careful with sensitive details. Gender, sexuality, age and state are shown on the board once someone is seated. Ask permission, and remove anyone who asks.
  • Backups: download one after each stream and keep it somewhere private.
