**How it works**
1. Open the link and sign in with Google.
2. Watch the board update live: who's in the hot seat, who's on the panel, and how well they fit (green, yellow or red).
3. Stuck for a question? Tap **Random question for me**.

**Using it for questions while you're in the hot seat or on the panel?**
Please use a **second device** (like your phone while you stream on your computer), or **write your questions down ahead of time**. Looking at the screen takes you away from your date, and we'd rather you spend that time talking. 💬

**Good to know**
• It's view only. You can't change anything on stream.
• Your random question is private to your screen.
• 18+ only.
• Tip: tap **Add** on the install card to put it on your home screen like an app.
• No Gmail? Any email works if you have a Google account with it. You can make one at accounts.google.com/signup and choose "Use my existing email address".
• The room holds 500 at a time. If you see **Full**, tap the tag and try again in a minute.
• We only use your email to let you in. Privacy and Terms are linked at the bottom of the page.
